Our paper on observation-aligned two-stage domain decomposition for physics-informed traffic state estimation is published in [**Transportation Research Part C: Emerging Technologies**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2026.105995). The method reconstructs freeway speed fields from sparse fixed sensors while preserving shockwaves.
